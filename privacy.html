Information We Collect

Most areas of the website can be used without creating an account. If you contact us, submit content, or use community features, we may collect information such as your username, email address, messages, or uploaded media.

How Information Is Used

Third-Party Services

Trackmania Events may use third-party services such as Supabase, Formspree, Twitch, YouTube, Discord, and GitHub Pages. These services may process limited technical or account-related information according to their own privacy policies.

Cookies & Local Storage

The site may use browser storage or cookies to keep users signed in, remember preferences, and improve overall functionality.

Content & Uploads

Any content submitted publicly, including shoutbox messages, highlights, or uploaded clips, may be visible to other users of the website.

Data Removal

If you would like content or account-related data removed, contact us and we will make reasonable efforts to handle the request.

Contact
